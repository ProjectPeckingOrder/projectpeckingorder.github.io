Terms of Use and Subscription Information

Updated 28 October 2026

Apple App Store

Pecking Order on the Apple App Store uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). This page provides information and links; it does not replace that agreement.

Google Play

For the Android app, the Google Play Terms of Service apply to your use of Google Play and purchases made through it. Apple's EULA applies to the Apple app.

Optional monthly subscription

Cloud backup and web access require an active monthly subscription in supported versions. The latest test versions also include additional breeder tools through Pecking Order Plus. Features depend on your installed version; the app explains what your subscription includes before purchase.

The app displays the store's current local price. The subscription renews monthly until cancelled. Your app store confirms the price, billing period and renewal terms before payment. Manage or cancel through the store account that made the purchase. Signing into Pecking Order or linking records does not start a subscription.

Existing permanent purchases remain yours after a subscription ends. Local phone records and manual backup files remain available without a cloud subscription. Cloud syncing and web editing require active cloud access.

Your records and privacy

Read the Pecking Order Privacy Policy for account sign-in, optional cloud records and photos, purchase verification, retention and deletion. Deleting a Pecking Order account does not cancel a store subscription; manage that through your store account.
