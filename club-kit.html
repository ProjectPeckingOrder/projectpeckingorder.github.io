For the person introducing the app

  1. Ask whether they want to start with birds, eggs or breeding.
  2. Use the fictional examples or the new in-app sample flock to show one short workflow.
  3. Let them make their first real entry. Optional fields can wait.
  4. Explain the plan relevant to their flock and how to save a backup.
