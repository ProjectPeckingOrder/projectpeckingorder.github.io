Before your first flock entry

A few useful answers.

Start free. Add the tools that suit your birds. These answers describe version 0.20.1 and the current web app; check your store for the version available to you.

What can I use for free?

Hobby includes up to 6 full chicken records and 4 full duck records, two custom fields, egg collection and statistics, egg-history CSV export, observed pecking-order relationships, basic chick batches and portable local backups. Duck options can be turned on in Settings. An account is not needed for these local phone features.

Retained full records count towards the allowance, including deceased, sold, rehomed and missing birds. Compare the plans.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. Use the free Hobby allowance or choose a one-time Home Breeder or Full Breeder purchase. Home Breeder adds unlimited individual records and breeder tools. Full Breeder also adds pen headcounts, movements and pen-specific records. Your one-time purchases do not expire.

Do I need to buy a breeder package before subscribing to Plus?

No. Pecking Order Plus includes all Home and Full Breeder tools while active, along with cloud backup, record and photo sync, web access and Breeding Projects. You do not need a separate breeder purchase. Breeding Projects is an early-access feature in development.

What happens if I cancel Plus or a preview ends?

Paid subscription access continues until the store-verified expiry; cancellation stops renewal. When access expires, Plus features, cloud sync and web editing stop. Your local flock records are kept and any one-time purchases remain yours. Retained records still count towards your remaining plan allowance, so adding records or using paid tools may require an upgrade.

You can still use portable backup files and download retained cloud backups after access ends, subject to the service's normal retention limits. Keep your own exported copies. See cloud history and recovery.

Can I use it without internet or an account?

Yes. Phone records and manual backups work locally. Internet is needed for store purchases, account sign-in and cloud access. Once linked, saved changes made offline wait on that device until the next successful sync. Finish a form using its Save or Record control; unfinished forms are not saved records.

How do I use my flock on a Mac, PC or tablet?

In a supported phone version, open Settings → Plus, cloud & web access, sign in and restore or activate your access. Link your phone records and let History & sync finish. Then sign in to the web app with the same email. A permanent breeder purchase alone does not include cloud or web access.

On a new installation, download the existing linked flock when offered. Read any replacement confirmation carefully. A cloud archive backup and live record sync are separate: check History & sync if another device has not updated.

Does an event-preview code include web access?

An eligible store-verified event preview can link web access in version 0.20.0 or later. Redeem the code in its matching store, restore purchases in the app and sign in with the email you will use on the web. The original verified 30-day preview expiry applies to both; signing in later does not restart it.

The event preview ends without automatic billing. Store eligibility and campaign dates still apply. Follow the redemption and linking steps.

Can I restore an older backup or move between phones?

The current app still reads supported older .peckingorder backups. In Settings, choose Restore backup and review the replacement warning. Version 0.20.0 and later saves a recovery copy before replacing the local records. Purchases are restored separately through the store that sold them; importing a backup does not transfer purchases between Apple and Google.

Are hatch reminders automatic? Do I have to candle?

Guided hatch monitoring is a breeder feature. Suggested dates follow the set date, species and incubation method; review them for your birds and care routine. Guided reminders start enabled, but your device must permit notifications and you can change or disable reminders. Candling is optional: record it if performed, and continue without it otherwise. Expected hatch dates are estimates.

Follow a breeding project from eggs to results.

Does the app predict genetics or measure my birds automatically?

No. You enter the birds, egg counts, observed relationships, parentage and breeding results. The app organises those observations into charts, maps and reports. A project helps you compare evidence across generations; it does not guarantee an inherited trait, egg colour or hatch outcome.

What usage information is collected?

The app asks whether you want to share anonymous usage. If you agree, it sends limited feature-use counts, flock-size ranges and recognised breed categories. It does not include bird names, notes, photos or account details in those reports. Change your choice in Settings. Cloud records are a separate, optional service. Read the privacy policy.
